What happened

In March I was travelling in Indonesia and got dengue fever. I was staying at Chacha Dormitory, a small family-run hostel in Ruteng, up in the hills of Flores.

Ayu and a doctor looked after me right there in the hostel. I was on a drip in my bunk. I don't know what I'd have done without them.

You can see from her guests' reviews that it wasn't just me. She looks after everyone.

What she asked for

When I got home I asked Ayu what I could do to say thank you. She didn't ask for anything for herself.

"Honestly we need more laptops for our class."Ayu, by WhatsApp

Ayu is a teacher. Next to the hostel she runs a small computer class for young people in Manggarai. Most of them can't go on to university. She teaches them computer skills so they can get jobs.

Several of the class laptops are broken and the rest are old. She asked the local government for help and heard nothing back.

What your money buys

A basic laptop in Ruteng costs about 7 million rupiah. That's about £300.
