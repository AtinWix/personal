How to play

  1. Cars come in at the white arrows (IN) and want the coloured exit (OUT) that matches their colour. Tap an entry to see its sign: where its cars go and how many.
  2. Most roads at the edge are two-way: an IN and an OUT side by side. Fat roads are busy, thin roads are quiet.
  3. Drag to draw a road. With Two-way on, one stroke builds both carriageways, and traffic keeps left. With it off, you build a one-way road in the direction you drag.
  4. To join roads, start or finish on an existing road. Crossing a road anywhere else builds a bridge over it.
  5. The Routes bar fills in a coloured square for each connection you've made. When every square is filled, press Run test.
  6. Line styles: Curve fits a smooth bend, Straight draws straight (snapping to 45°), Free follows your hand. Reshape bends a built road; tap it to straighten.
  7. Water costs ×3, bridges £3m. If traffic stops moving you'll see Jammed!. Erase removes a road; Restart wipes the level.
